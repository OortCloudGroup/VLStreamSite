Self-Developed VLS Protocol

Purpose-built for video IoT. Low latency, extreme packet loss resistance, and universal compatibility.

Video Transmission

  • VLS Protocol: TCP-based, <1.2s latency, no stutter at 30% loss
  • WebRTC P2P: Millisecond interconnection, 70% bandwidth savings
  • Multi-protocol: HLS, FLV, RTSP, GB28181 — all terminals
  • Encoding: H.264/H.265, 4K/1080p/720p adaptive

IoT & Control

  • MQTT: Transparent command transmission to devices
  • IoT Center: TCP/IP 4G network capabilities for peripherals
  • Object Model: Standardized device descriptions & sync
  • Fusion: Video stream + sensor data combined alerts

Security & Compliance

  • GB28181: National surveillance standard
  • GB35114: Enhanced security certification
  • Encryption: In-transit and at-rest data protection
  • Audit: Full operation logging & access control
